Bring in every book you've bought on Amazon

A small script reads your Kindle library on Amazon's own site, in your browser, signed in as you. You paste the result here. Amazon never talks to this site, and this site's server never sees your books.

  1. Add the buttonDrag 📚 Kindle → findMyNextBook to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Click it on AmazonOpen read.amazon.com/kindle-library, sign in, and click the bookmark. It copies your library.
  3. Paste it hereClick the box below and press ⌘V (Ctrl+V), or drop the file it offers to download.
No bookmarks bar? Use the browser console instead
On read.amazon.com/kindle-library, open the developer console (⌥⌘J in Chrome, ⌥⌘C in Safari), paste this and press Enter. Chrome may ask you to type “allow pasting” first. 

          

        

        

          What exactly does the script do?
          
It runs on Amazon's page and makes two kinds of request, both to Amazon itself: the list of your Kindle books (on read.amazon.com) or the dates Kindle saw you finish them (on amazon.com). It puts the result on your clipboard and offers it as a download. It sends nothing anywhere else. The full source is the code under “browser console” above.
