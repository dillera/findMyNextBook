Import every book you've bought on Amazon

Your Kindle library joins your library here, and every book in it counts as read. Goodreads ratings stay attached, and you'll never be recommended a book you already own.

  1. Chrome opens on AmazonIt uses this app's own Chrome profile, separate from your everyday browser.
  2. You sign in onceThe session is saved on this Mac, so later imports run in the background.
  3. Your Kindle library is readTitles, covers and the dates Kindle saw you finish. It stays on this machine.
Can't use Chrome here? Paste your library instead
  1. Open read.amazon.com/kindle-library in any browser and sign in.
  2. Open the developer console (⌥⌘J in Chrome) and run this. It copies your whole Kindle library to the clipboard.
